# Overview

Claude Blog Brain is a persistent operating brain for blog content creation, optimization, and management dual-optimized for Google rankings (E-E-A-T, the 2026 core updates) and AI citations (GEO/AEO), spanning writing, rewriting and freshness, SERP-informed briefs and outlines, editorial calendars and strategy, semantic topic clusters, schema and internal linking, multilingual publishing, the FLOW framework, factchecking, personas, distribution, and the blog delivery contract, grounded in the claude-blog skill.
