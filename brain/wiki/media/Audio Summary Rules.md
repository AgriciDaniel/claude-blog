# Audio Summary Rules

## Audio Summary Rules Rule Scope

Audio Summary Rules governs short spoken versions of blog posts: embedded audio recaps, podcast snippets, social audio cuts, narrated abstracts, and audio tracks paired with video. The rule is not "make a livelier script." The rule is "preserve the article's claim boundary while making the listening experience useful."

The quality baseline comes from people-first content guidance in `g-helpful-content`. Google AI guidance in `g-ai-opt-guide` prevents the summary from becoming a hidden AI-only asset with different claims from the page. Video guidance in `g-video` applies when the audio is attached to a visible video or video landing page. Schema.org vocabulary in `schema-full` may name AudioObject or transcript properties, but support in a vocabulary does not create Google feature eligibility by itself. See [[Blog Schema Stack]] before adding markup.

### Allowed Actions And Disallowed Actions

- Allowed: compress the article's answer, preserve caveats, identify source dates, and point listeners back to the canonical article.
- Allowed: omit examples that are not necessary for comprehension.
- Disallowed: add a statistic, promise, recommendation, or product comparison absent from the article.
- Disallowed: turn a contested claim into a confident narrated line.
- Disallowed: use audio as a replacement for visible source citations.

## Audio Summary Rules Review And Rollback

1. Build a two-column comparison: article claim on the left, audio line on the right.
2. Strike audio lines that introduce new facts, dates, guarantees, or unsupported urgency.
3. Confirm the transcript or show notes link to the canonical article and source IDs.
4. If the article changes materially, mark the audio asset stale until the script is reread.
5. Roll back the audio summary when a source is corrected, a claim verdict changes, or the audio cannot expose the needed caveat.

## Audio Summary Rules Edge Cases

When a summary is generated by a tool, route provenance and disclosure to [[Generated Media Disclosure Notes]]. When the audio uses a chart claim, require [[Chart Source Requirements]] first. When the audio is only a distribution variant, [[Media Repurposing Matrix]] controls channel length and link targets.

## Narrated Source Recap

A 90-second recap summarizes a guide on blog refresh decisions.
Before review, the script says "this update will recover rankings."
That guarantee is absent from the article and fails `g-helpful-content`.
The revised line says the article lists refresh signals and rollback notes.
It names the source date only where the article already names it.
The show notes link the canonical article and visible source packet.
If the page adds a video player, `g-video` triggers transcript alignment.

## Audio-Only Failure Points

- A sponsor read cannot smuggle a product comparison into the recap.
- A stale source correction reopens every downloadable audio file.
- A generated voice must not sound like a quoted expert.
- Background music cannot cover the source caveat in the spoken claim.
- Podcast feed notes do not replace visible citations on the article page.
