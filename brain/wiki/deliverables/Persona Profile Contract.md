# Persona Profile Contract

## Persona Profile Deliverable Boundary

This contract defines what a persona profile must contain before writers use it. The profile translates audience evidence into readability, tone, vocabulary, decision context, summary labels, and writing constraints. It supports [[Voice and Style]] and does not replace a content brief, brand strategy, or legal review.

### Required Inputs And Exclusions

Inputs are audience segment, knowledge level, task urgency, topic sensitivity, preferred vocabulary, forbidden claims, sample pages, and source IDs that support reader needs. `g-helpful-content` keeps the persona tied to useful content rather than demographic stereotypes. `g-qrg-full` supports care around sensitive topics, especially when user harm or trust risk is present.

### Voice Fields The Contract Must Produce

The profile must output reading level, tone dimensions, vocabulary to use, vocabulary to avoid, evidence tolerance, example intro style, CTA comfort, accessibility notes, and escalation rules. `nng-editorial-heuristics` can inform usability of labels and review flow. `g-ai-opt-guide` prevents persona notes from drifting into unsupported AI-visibility tactics.

## Persona Profile Contract Acceptance Table

| Persona field | Required evidence | Validator | Acceptance criterion | Handoff owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reader task | Actual job, pain, or decision | Strategist | Persona can guide section priorities | Content strategist |
| Knowledge level | Familiar terms and unknown terms | Editor | Draft can choose examples without guessing | Editor |
| Tone dimensions | Directness, warmth, authority, caution | Brand reviewer | Voice is concrete enough to apply | Brand owner |
| Vocabulary rules | Approved words and banned phrases | Editor | Terms reduce ambiguity and risk | Writer |
| Trust sensitivity | YMYL, reputation, or expert-review need | E-E-A-T reviewer | Sensitive topics have escalation rule | Reviewer |
| Evidence tolerance | Source depth expected by reader | Researcher | Claims match reader risk and skepticism | Researcher |

## Handoff Procedure For Writers

1. Attach the persona profile to briefs before outline creation.
2. Translate tone dimensions into examples, not vague adjectives.
3. Reject persona instructions that encourage unsupported claims or hide important caveats.
4. Revisit the profile when audience evidence, product positioning, or content risk changes.

## Source IDs Used

Persona profiles use `g-helpful-content`, `g-qrg-full`, `nng-editorial-heuristics`, and `g-ai-opt-guide`.
