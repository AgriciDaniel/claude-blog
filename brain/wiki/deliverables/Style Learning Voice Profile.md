# Style Learning Voice Profile

## Distinct Style Learning Job

This profile turns approved writing samples into measurable voice guidance for [[Voice and Style]]. It captures sentence cadence, vocabulary richness, passive voice tolerance, transition density, signature phrases, and prohibited habits. It is not a license to imitate an unapproved person or to override factual clarity. The source IDs are `g-helpful-content`, `nng-editorial-heuristics`, `g-qrg-full`, and `g-ai-opt-guide`.

## Inputs Specific To Voice Sampling

Use only brand-approved samples, audience context, channel, locale, excluded phrases, and reviewer notes. Mark any sample that is old, campaign-specific, ghostwritten, or legally constrained. A profile created from thin samples must remain advisory until the brand owner approves examples.

## Style Decisions To Record

| Decision | Required input | Evidence state | Owner | Next action |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Sentence cadence | Three to five approved excerpts | Average length and rhythm note | Voice reviewer | Approve range |
| Vocabulary richness | Domain terms and banned terms | Glossary match and plain-language check | Editor | Update `VOICE.md` |
| Passive voice | Sample allowance and trust context | Use only when agency is not hidden | Factchecker | Flag unclear actor |
| Transition rate | Paragraph samples | Too many formulaic bridges marked | Editor | Rewrite sample |
| Signature phrases | Approved phrase list | Phrase source and use case named | Brand owner | Limit frequency |
| Trust posture | Author, review, disclosure needs | QRG-sensitive topics flagged | Reviewer | Escalate if needed |
| AI drafting boundary | Workflow and disclosure context | No special AI visibility claim | Operator | Route to FLOW |

## Operating Procedure For Updates

1. Compare a draft paragraph against the profile and identify only actionable differences.
2. Preserve helpfulness and trust over mimicry when style guidance conflicts with clarity.
3. Record any phrase ban with an example, because abstract taste notes decay quickly.
4. Revalidate the profile after brand repositioning, locale expansion, or a major audience change.
