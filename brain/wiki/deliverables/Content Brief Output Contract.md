# Content Brief Output Contract

## Content Brief Boundary For SERP Informed Planning

This contract defines the deliverable produced before an outline or article draft exists. A content brief must describe search intent, audience, competitor patterns, statistics, source constraints, image or chart opportunities, and handoff notes. It belongs beside [[SERP-Informed Briefs and Outlines]] and does not decide final wording, schema markup, or publication timing.

### Inputs That Cannot Be Skipped

The brief needs a target topic, target locale, audience segment, query set, current SERP observations, internal-link candidates, approved source IDs, and any brand or compliance limits. `g-helpful-content` sets the usefulness standard; `g-qrg-full` guides trust sensitivity when the topic can affect money, safety, or wellbeing.

### Exclusions From The Brief

The brief must not copy competitor structure, forecast traffic from market studies, or treat zero-click research as property-specific performance. `sparktoro-zero-click-2026` can explain search-journey context only when labeled as AS-REPORTED through [[Dual Optimization]].

## Required Brief Sections

Each brief must include reader job, intent map, article promise, required and excluded claims, source pack, section jobs, media recommendations, internal-link zones, and draft risks. AI-facing notes must stay within `g-ai-opt-guide` and link to [[AI Citation Mechanics]] when extraction or citation language appears.

## Content Brief Output Contract Acceptance Table

| Brief field | Required detail | Validator | Acceptance criterion | Handoff owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Reader job | User, task, desired outcome | Strategist | Reader need is specific enough to draft against | Content strategist |
| SERP pattern | Observed formats, dates, locale | SEO analyst | Observation is dated and not called a ranking factor | SEO analyst |
| Evidence pack | Source IDs, verdict labels, caveats | Researcher | Current claims have nearby sources | Researcher |
| Media prompt | Chart or image purpose, data need | Media editor | Visual recommendation is useful and sourced | Media editor |
| AI note | Extraction-friendly section needs | GEO reviewer | No special AI file or guarantee is requested | GEO owner |
| Draft handoff | Must include, must avoid, unresolved blockers | Editor | Writer can draft without inventing facts | Editor |

## Handoff To Outline And Draft

1. Approve the brief only after source IDs are mapped to claim slots.
2. Move heading hierarchy decisions to the outline artifact.
3. Send unresolved trust, AI, or media questions back to the owner named in the acceptance table.

## Source IDs Used

Content brief evidence uses `g-helpful-content`, `g-ai-opt-guide`, `sparktoro-zero-click-2026`, and `g-qrg-full`.
