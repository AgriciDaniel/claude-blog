# Repurposing Asset Matrix

## Repurposing Asset Matrix Comparison Job

This matrix translates a source article into channel-specific assets for social, email, video, podcast, and community use while protecting claim fidelity. It belongs with [[Distribution and Repurposing]] and should never turn a caveated source claim into a broad promotional promise.

### Rows This Matrix Must Contain

Rows should cover the original article, LinkedIn post, short social post, email summary, video outline, podcast prompt, community discussion, and AI-search snippet candidate. `similarweb-gen-ai-stats-2026` can support market context about AI referral growth only with vendor-study caveats. `blog-io2026` supports Google's own AI Mode scale announcement, not a promise of traffic from repurposed content.

### Columns That Make Decisions Auditable

Columns must record channel intent, asset format, source claim carried over, required caveat, link target, owner, approval state, and measurement signal. `g-ai-features` defines Google AI Search surfaces and preview controls; `sparktoro-zero-click-2026` is a market-context source routed through [[AI Citation Mechanics]] or [[Dual Optimization]].

## Cross Platform Asset Matrix

| Asset row | Channel intent | Fidelity check | Required caveat | Link target | Owner | Approval state |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Original article | Full evidence and conversion support | Source IDs stay nearest to claims | None beyond article caveats | Canonical URL | Editor | draft, approved, or blocked |
| LinkedIn post | Professional summary | Statistics retain source date | Practitioner or vendor caveat when used | Article or hub | Social lead | draft, approved, or blocked |
| Email summary | Owned-audience recap | No unsupported urgency | Explain what changed and why | Article or signup page | Lifecycle owner | draft, approved, or blocked |
| Video outline | Explain concept visually | Spoken claim matches article wording | Do not imply guaranteed AI visibility | Article URL | Video owner | draft, approved, or blocked |
| Community prompt | Start discussion | Question does not distort evidence | Label open questions as questions | Discussion thread | Community lead | draft, approved, or blocked |
| AI snippet candidate | Tight answer passage | Source and entity remain clear | No inclusion guarantee | Article section | GEO owner | draft, approved, or blocked |

## Interpretation Rules For Channel Owners

1. Preserve the article's strongest caveat when shortening a claim.
2. Choose the channel objective before writing copy.
3. Keep measurement channel-specific: clicks, replies, listens, views, or cited exposure are not interchangeable.
