# Blog Strategy Architecture Blueprint

## Strategy Blueprint Planning Scope

This blueprint defines the operating shape of a blog program: positioning, audience, topic clusters, differentiation, AI citation surfaces, measurement, and source governance. It does not create a content calendar by itself and does not guarantee ranking or AI visibility. The blueprint links strategy choices to [[Semantic Topic Clusters]], [[Dual Optimization]], and [[AI Citation Mechanics]].

### Positioning Inputs

Required inputs are brand promise, audience segments, product or service priorities, existing content inventory, competitor pattern notes, source constraints, and measurement availability. `g-helpful-content` anchors usefulness and originality. `g-ai-opt-guide` prevents the strategy from inventing special Google AI requirements.

### Open Decisions That Block Architecture

Blockers include unclear audience ownership, no primary topic cluster, unsupported AI citation promise, missing measurement source, and no differentiation beyond keyword coverage. `sparktoro-zero-click-2026` and `seer-aio-impact-ctr-2026` can inform market pressure, but both remain practitioner or market context rather than client-specific proof.

## Blog Strategy Architecture Blueprint Execution Table

| Architecture phase | Strategic question | Evidence to inspect | Output | Owner | Review trigger |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Positioning | Why should this blog win trust? | Brand facts, author proof, `g-helpful-content` | Positioning thesis | Strategy lead | Audience or product shift |
| Cluster design | Which hubs and spokes create depth? | Inventory and intent map | Cluster architecture | Content strategist | New hub or cannibalization risk |
| Differentiation | What information gain exists? | Examples, data, expert input | Unique angle requirements | Editor | Drafts become generic |
| AI surface planning | Which passages deserve extraction readiness? | `g-ai-opt-guide`, [[AI Citation Mechanics]] | GEO advisory rules | GEO owner | Google AI doc update |
| Measurement | What can be observed? | GSC, AI report access, market studies | Reporting plan with caveats | Analyst | Data source changes |

## Measurement And Differentiation Loop

1. Convert positioning into cluster rules before assigning individual briefs.
2. Require each planned asset to state its information gain compared with existing coverage.
3. Separate official Google AI guidance from third-party CTR or zero-click studies in every strategy recommendation.
4. Reopen the blueprint when data availability, product positioning, or Google AI guidance changes.
