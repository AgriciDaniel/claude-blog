# Start Here

## Summary

Claude Blog Brain is a source-cited Obsidian brain for blog content creation, optimization, and management.

It is dual optimized for Google rankings and AI citations, but it does not promise rankings, traffic, or AI inclusion.

It is advisory and read-only toward CMS, Google Search Console, GA4, publishing systems, and external platforms.
