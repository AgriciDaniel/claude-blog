## Verdict Rules

- `CONFIRMED`: directly supported by an official, primary, standards, or first-party source.
- `AS-REPORTED`: a dated study or vendor report says this, but the claim should retain that source's methodology and scope.
- `SINGLE-SOURCE`: one dated source supports the claim and no corroborating source is available in the ledger.
- `CONTESTED`: credible sources conflict, the source does not prove the operational claim, or the claim is a common SEO/GEO overreach.

## Verification Flow

1. Draft the claim in plain language.
2. Attack what the source does not prove.
3. Assign `confidence`, `evidence_tier`, and `verdict`.
4. Add a second source for market, traffic, ranking, AI visibility, or named-site impact claims.
5. Record limits before the claim is used in a deliverable.
