## Intended Buyer (Product Hypothesis)

The intended buyer is a content team, blogger, SEO content strategist, or operator
already using the claude-blog skill and needing a persistent, cited operating
brain for blog writing, rewriting, audits, schema, topic clusters, multilingual
publishing, GEO and AEO, FLOW evidence discipline, and distribution.

## Search Behavior Shift

Claim: the current StatCounter view is September 2026, worldwide, with
platform filters. SparkToro separately estimates zero-click behavior in a US
January-April 2026 desktop/mobile-web panel. Neither dataset measures all
users or proves a particular blog's traffic change.
Source: StatCounter search engine market share, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Source: SparkToro zero-click study, 2026-06-08, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: medium. Evidence tier: PRACTITIONER for dynamic market-share and
clickstream studies.
Market implication: blog programs need to measure brand exposure, citation
visibility, and assisted demand in addition to organic clicks.

Claim: SparkToro estimates 68.01% zero-click searches in its weighted US
January-April 2026 desktop/mobile-web panel. Treat extrapolation to other
markets or channels as uncertain.
Source: SparkToro, 2026-06-08, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: medium. Evidence tier: PRACTITIONER.
Market implication: briefs need answer-first sections, source blocks, and
conversion paths for readers who arrive after seeing the answer elsewhere.

## AI Overviews And AI Mode

Claim: AI Overviews changed click economics, but the measured effect varies by
study, query class, brand citation status, and data window.
Sources: Seer Interactive 2026 AIO CTR update, 2026-04-24; Pew Research Center,
2025-07-22; Ahrefs, 2026-02-04; retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: medium. Evidence tier: CONTESTED for universal CTR loss,
PRACTITIONER for individual benchmarks.
Product hypothesis: customers may value citation-readiness audits that distinguish
ranking, being cited, and post-click conversion.

Claim: Google announced over 1B monthly AI Mode users in May 2026.
SparkToro separately measured 0.34% of US Google searches in its January-April
2026 panel. User reach and query share use different populations and units.
Source: Google I/O Search update, 2026-05-19, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Source: SparkToro, 2026-06-08, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high for Google user count, medium for behavior share. Evidence
tier: EVIDENCE-BASED for Google, PRACTITIONER for SparkToro.
Market implication: AI Mode should be represented in strategy and reporting,
but it should not displace core Google, AI Overview, and content quality work.

Claim: Search Console launched generative-AI impressions reports on June 3
for a subset. Google's August 31 update says these insights rolled out to all
websites worldwide. August 13-17 missing generative-AI logging data was
restored on August 21 according to the official anomalies log.
Source: Google Search Central, Search Generative AI performance reports,
2026-06-03, updated 2026-08-31, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Reporting recovery: `gsc-reporting-anomalies-2026-08-13`,
<https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/6211453?hl=en>, reviewed 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high. Evidence tier: EVIDENCE-BASED.
Market implication: the brain should prepare reports for AI Overview and AI Mode
impressions when a client has access to the data.

## GEO And AEO Demand

Claim: Ahrefs observed limited cited-URL overlap between AI Overviews and AI
Mode; seoClarity measured Google visibility for ChatGPT-cited URLs in its
tracked keyword set. Defining citation visibility as a
separate deliverable is a product choice, not demonstrated buyer demand.
Source: Ahrefs AI Overviews vs AI Mode, 2025-12-15, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Source: seoClarity ChatGPT cited pages analysis, 2025-11-20, retrieved
2026-10-07.
Confidence: medium. Evidence tier: PRACTITIONER; methodology is vendor or
practitioner analysis, not first-party client data.
Market implication: content teams need URL-level and passage-level guidance, not
only keyword rankings.

Claim: Google does not require special AI files, special AI schema, or
`llms.txt` for Search AI features.
Sources: `g-ai-opt-guide` page last updated 2026-07-10, retrieved 2026-10-07;
`g-update-2026-06-15-llms-txt-clarified-as-unused-by-google-search` changelog
event 2026-06-15, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high. Evidence tier: EVIDENCE-BASED.
Market implication: the product should protect buyers from unsupported GEO
tactics and keep recommendations grounded in content quality, crawlability, and
structured entity clarity.

Claim: Similarweb estimated worldwide generative-AI website-visit share
changed from about 76% ChatGPT in June 2025 to about 53% in May 2026, while
Gemini reached about 27-28%. This is modeled website use, excluding native-app
activity, and does not measure outbound referral growth to a client site.
Source: `similarweb-gen-ai-stats-2026`, article dated 2026-07-29, retrieved
2026-10-07, <https://aisearch.similarweb.com/blog/gen-ai-stats/>.
Confidence: medium. Evidence tier: PRACTITIONER.
Market implication: distinguish platform website-visit share from client
referrals, referral growth, and conversion attribution.

## Competitive Tooling Landscape

Claim: Google Search Console remains the first-party truth source for Search
performance, while third-party SEO tools cannot access Google's internal ranking
data.
Source: Google guidance on third-party SEO tools, services, and advice,
2026-06-05, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high. Evidence tier: EVIDENCE-BASED.
Market implication: this brain should ingest and cite third-party data as
supporting evidence, not as ground truth.

Claim: official and vendor documentation describes APIs for GSC, PageSpeed
Insights, CrUX, GA4, keyword planning, crawling, and SERP data. These capability
docs do not establish buyer adoption or integration success.
Sources: Search Console API, PageSpeed Insights API, CrUX API, GA4 Data API,
Google Ads Keyword Planning API, DataForSEO API, Ahrefs API, Firecrawl docs, all
retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high for official and vendor API capability docs.
Market implication: the adapter plan should support blog post input first, then
add optional connectors for GSC, PSI, CrUX, GA4, SERP, and crawl data.

Claim: schema tooling remains fragmented between Google eligibility tests,
Schema.org vocabulary, and JSON-LD validation.
Sources: Google structured data introduction, Google Search Gallery, Schema.org,
W3C JSON-LD 1.1, retrieved 2026-10-07.
Confidence: high. Evidence tier: EVIDENCE-BASED.
Market implication: the brain should provide schema recommendations as
eligibility and understanding signals, never guaranteed rich results.

## Buying Trigger (Product Hypothesis)

The proposed trigger is risk reduction. The intended audience may need a durable place where
volatile Google policy, AI citation claims, schema deprecations, FLOW evidence
rules, and the claude-blog delivery contract stay aligned. The product is
valuable when it prevents stale advice, fabricated statistics, unsupported GEO
claims, and delivery of drafts that fail the blog quality gates.
