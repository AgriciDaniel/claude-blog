## Core Thesis

Google's helpful content guidance gives self assessment questions for content quality, originality, expertise, trust, and people-first intent. For blog work, it is the main official source for rejecting thin aggregation and unsupported summaries.

## Blog Application

- Require original value beyond summarizing existing search results.
- Ask whether a reader would leave satisfied without another search.
- Make authorship, expertise, sourcing, and update practices visible.
- Use AI as an assisted workflow only when human accountability and quality checks remain.
