Google's August 28, 2026 documentation records an EEA-specific enforcement
adjustment for site reputation abuse. Preserve that regional scope. The
underlying policy still applies.

## Blog Application

- Reject mass AI pages without added value.
- Reject third-party parasite content intended to exploit host reputation.
- Require human review, original value, and source provenance before publication.
- Treat spam policy risk as higher priority than on-page optimization.
