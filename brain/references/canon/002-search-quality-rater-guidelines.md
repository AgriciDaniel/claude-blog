## Core Thesis

The Search Quality Rater Guidelines define the human rating framework for Page Quality and Needs Met. They are not direct ranking inputs, but they document what Google asks raters to evaluate: purpose, main content quality, E-E-A-T, reputation, trust, and YMYL risk.

## Blog Application

- Grade content against its stated purpose.
- Escalate YMYL or YMYL-adjacent posts for expert review.
- Treat author attribution, source citations, reputation, and transparency as trust requirements.
- Keep AI-generated or AI-assisted content accountable to the same quality bar as human-written content.
